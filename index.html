THE FAILURE MODE

1

Action leaves your process

An agent asks another system to mutate something.

2

Acknowledgement disappears

The request times out after the external system may already have changed.

3

Naive retry duplicates work

Treating silence as failure can create a second write, deploy, payment or message.

4

Read reality first

Observed post-state decides whether success or retry is safe.

WHAT THIS PROVES

Small enough to inspect. Strict enough to fail closed.

The public lab demonstrates one invariant. It is not the private MachineOutcome runtime and it does not claim customer adoption, ROI or universal agent reliability.

01

Bind evidence

Wrong-task and wrong-attempt evidence cannot prove the action.

02

Bind pre-state

A stale starting state blocks the mutation boundary.

03

Read after ambiguity

Transport timeout does not decide the real outcome.

04

Keep UNKNOWN

Missing or ambiguous reality stays unresolved instead of being guessed into success or failure.
